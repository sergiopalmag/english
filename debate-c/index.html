What we learned今天學到的

  • A debate is a formal discussion: rules, turns and judges.辯論是正式的討論：有規則、輪流發言、有評審。
  • The Affirmative agrees. The Negative disagrees.正方同意，反方不同意。
  • Our resolutions are about policy: “Students should …”我們的辯題是政策性的：「學生應該……」
  • Everything happens on Google Classroom and the Team Board.所有事情都在 Google Classroom 和 Team Board 上。
