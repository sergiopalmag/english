Unit 2 · Reasons for your opinionp. 8

Opinions need reasons意見需要理由

Opinions do not stand up by themselves. Give reasons that other people find reasonable, not just you.意見不能單獨成立，要提出別人也覺得合理的理由。
OpinionLiving in the city is better than living in the country …
+ because… because getting to and from work is easier in the city.
= a reason others can checkNot “because I like it.” A reason must work for most people.
Unit 2 · Strong reasonsp. 9

A strong reason is …有力的理由要……

Logical合乎邏輯

It really supports the opinion.

Cats are better than dogs because hot dogs have mustard. ✗

Specific and clear具體清楚

It states one clear idea.

The country is better because it has more good things. ✗

Convincing說服大多數人

Most people would agree it matters.

The country is better than the city because I like it. ✗

Judges decide if a reason is strong or weak.評審會判斷理由是強還是弱。
Unit 2 · Practicep. 9

You are the judge你是評審

Which reason is strongest?
Show a, b or c with your fingers.
Cats are better than dogs …
abecause cats can see better in the dark.
bbecause cats are cleaner.
cbecause hot dogs have mustard and cats don't.
Being clean matters to most pet owners. Seeing in the dark doesn't help you as an owner, and hot dogs aren't dogs.
It is better to live in the country than the city …
abecause the country has more good things.
bbecause I like it.
cbecause the environment is cleaner.
“More good things” is not specific, and “I like it” only convinces you.
Teenagers should not smoke …
abecause it makes their breath smell bad.
bbecause I don't like to smoke.
cbecause many people who begin smoking as teenagers later develop cancer.
Cancer is a serious, specific result that convinces most people.
The rainforests of the world should be protected …
abecause rainforests are natural.
bbecause rainforests produce oxygen.
cbecause trees are needed for wood to build houses.
Oxygen is a clear, important reason. Wood for houses is a reason to cut trees down.

Period 6 · Unit 3 · Supporting your reasonsp. 12

Opinion → Reason → Support意見 → 理由 → 支持證據

Opinion“In my opinion, the country is much better than the city …”
Reason“… because there are more fun things to do in the country.”
Support“For example, in the country you can go fishing, hiking, rock climbing and camping.”
A reason without support is just another opinion. Support is the evidence that makes judges believe you.沒有證據的理由只是另一個意見。支持證據讓評審相信你。
Unit 3 · Types of supportpp. 13–14

Four kinds of support四種支持證據

Explanation解釋

Explain how or why it happens.

Cats are independent, so …

Example例子

A real case, often from your own life.

For example, my cat can …

Statistic統計數字

A number, a percent, a fact you can count.

20% of all students …

Expert opinion專家意見

What a doctor, scientist or report says.

According to Dr. Kittilove, …
Use two different types for each reason. A statistic + an example is stronger than two examples.每個理由用兩種不同類型的證據。

Unit 4 · Your argumentp. 19

Every point has three parts每個論點有三個部分

1 · Signpost2 · Reason3 · Supports

Our first point is cost. Soccer tickets are much cheaper than baseball tickets. In this city, the price of a ticket to a baseball game is about three times higher than the price of a ticket to a soccer game.

Our second point is excitement. Soccer is much more exciting than baseball. In baseball, 90% of the time the players are just standing around waiting. Soccer has action all the time.

This is exactly what you write for homework, one block per reason.回家作業就是照這個格式寫，一個理由一段。

Period 5 · Delivery

Speak like a debater像辯手一樣說話

Do要

  • Stand still and tall. Feet on the floor.
  • Look at three places: left, middle, right.
  • Show the number with your fingers: “Our first reason …”
  • Pause after each signpost.
  • Speak to the back row.

Don't不要

  • Read every word from your paper.
  • Use your phone.
  • Rush to finish.
  • Turn your back to the audience.
  • Use words you can't pronounce.
Period 6 · Cue cards

Key words, not sentences提示卡：只寫關鍵字

② COST fish = cheap goldfish + tank ≈ NT$600 NT$20 each → easy ③ STRESS pets → relax Am. Heart Assoc.
  • One card for each reason. Number them.每個理由一張卡，標上號碼。
  • Write the signpost big. Then 3–4 key words.路標寫大一點，再寫 3–4 個關鍵字。
  • Numbers and names go on the card, so you get them right.數字和名字寫在卡上，避免說錯。
  • Practice until you only need to glance down.練到只需要偶爾看一下。
