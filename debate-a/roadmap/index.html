Each debate takes about 23 minutes: 8 speeches of up to 18 minutes, plus changeovers and scoring.

Debate Day rules

辯論日規則

Speaking order and time limits發言順序與時間

1Aff 1st2:30
2Neg 1st2:30
3Aff 2nd2:00
4Neg 2nd2:00
5Aff 3rd2:00
6Neg 3rd2:00
7Neg Final2:30
8Aff Final2:30

The Affirmative team wants the change, so it speaks first and last (p. 28). Teams of 3: if both teams have 3, skip the 3rd speeches. If only one team has 3, one person gives two speeches.正方（同意的一方）第一個發言也最後一個發言。三人隊伍：如果兩隊都是三人，就省略第三位講者；如果只有一隊是三人，由一位隊員講兩次。

Green card: start
Yellow card: 30 seconds left
Red card: time's up, finish your sentence

Speaker roles講者角色

  • 1stIntroduces the resolution and lists all of the team's signposts. Then presents their own 2 reasons.
  • 2ndPresents their own 2 reasons, each with supports.
  • 3rdPresents their own 2 reasons, each with supports.
  • FinalPresents their own 2 reasons, then summarizes every teammate's points and closes for the team.

Teamwork rules團隊合作規則

  • One reason, one owner. Never write a teammate's reason.每個理由只有一位負責人，不要寫隊友的理由。
  • Check the Team Board before you write. See what your teammates already did.動筆前先看 Team Board，確認隊友寫了什麼。
  • Your argument is yours. Introductions can be the same; arguments can't.開場白可以一樣，論點不能一樣。
  • Help, don't copy. Give teammates ideas and feedback. Every word in your speech must be one you understand.可以幫忙，但不能抄。演講稿裡的每個字你都要懂。
