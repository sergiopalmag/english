Debate Day rules

辯論日規則

Speaking order and time limits發言順序與時間

1Aff 1st正方一辯2:30
2Neg 1st反方一辯2:30
3Aff 2nd正方二辯2:00
4Neg 2nd反方二辯2:00
5Aff 3rd正方三辯2:00
6Neg 3rd反方三辯2:00
7Neg Final反方結辯2:30
8Aff Final正方結辯2:30

The Affirmative team wants the change, so it speaks first and last (p. 28). Teams of 3: if both teams have 3, skip the 3rd speeches. If only one team has 3, one person gives two speeches.正方（同意的一方）第一個發言，也最後一個發言。三人隊伍：如果兩隊都是三人，就省略第三位講者；如果只有一隊是三人，由一位隊員講兩次。

Green card: start綠牌：開始
Yellow card: 30 seconds left黃牌：剩 30 秒
Red card: time’s up, finish your sentence紅牌：時間到，說完這句話

Speaker roles講者角色

  • 1st一辯Introduces the resolution and lists all of the team’s signposts. Then presents their own 2 reasons.介紹辯題，列出全隊所有的路標，再說自己的 2 個理由。
  • 2nd二辯Presents their own 2 reasons, each with supports.說自己的 2 個理由，每個都有證據。
  • 3rd三辯Presents their own 2 reasons, each with supports.說自己的 2 個理由，每個都有證據。
  • Final結辯Presents their own 2 reasons, then summarizes every teammate’s points and closes for the team.說自己的 2 個理由，再整理所有隊友的論點並做結論。

Teamwork rules團隊合作規則

  • One reason, one owner. Never write a teammate’s reason.每個理由只有一位負責人，不要寫隊友的理由。
  • Check the Team Board before you write. See what your teammates already did.動筆前先看 Team Board，確認隊友寫了什麼。
  • Your argument is yours. Introductions can be the same; arguments can’t.開場白可以一樣，論點不能一樣。
  • Help, don’t copy. Give teammates ideas and feedback. Every word in your speech must be one you understand.可以幫忙，但不能抄。講稿裡的每個字你都要懂。

How each speaker is scored評分方式

Category · 項目Points · 分數
Used eye contact眼神接觸0–2
Used gestures手勢0–2
Spoke loud and clear聲音大又清楚0–3
Easy to understand容易聽懂0–3
Strong arguments論點有力0–5
Total per speaker每位講者總分15
