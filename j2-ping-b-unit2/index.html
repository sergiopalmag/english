How to Write to Agree or Disagree

如何寫同意或不同意的意見文
Tell if you agree or disagree with the statement.說出你同意還是不同意這個說法。
Give reasons and examples for your opinion.為你的意見提出理由和例子。
You can say what many people think, then why you agree or disagree.可以先說很多人的想法，再說你為什麼同意或不同意。
Rule 3 in action · 第三點的例子
Many people think that money makes people happy. However, I disagree that money is the most important thing.

Pattern 1 · 句型一

I Agree / Disagree That …

表達同意或不同意
Agree · 同意
I agree that…
I believe that…
Disagree · 不同意
I disagree that…
I don't agree that…
I don't believe that…
After that, write a full sentence: who + verb.that 後面要接完整的句子：主詞 + 動詞。
Examples · 例句
I believe that every student should learn to swim.
I don't agree that phones make people less friendly.
I disagree that bubble tea is better than water because it has too much sugar.
Watch out · 常見錯誤
✗ I am agree that…✓ I agree that…
✗ I disagree with that school is boring.✓ I disagree that school is boring.

Pattern 3 · 句型三

Even Though …, …

雖然……，但是……
Even though idea A, idea B.
Use it for two ideas that seem opposite. Idea B is the important one.用在兩個看起來相反的想法。B 是比較重要的想法。
Even though it was raining, we played basketball.
Even though bubble tea is sweet, many people drink it every day.
We played basketball even though it was raining. (no comma)
Watch out · 常見錯誤
✗ Even though it was raining, but we played.✓ Even though it was raining, we played.
✗ Even though it was raining we played.✓ Add a comma: raining, we played.
In English, Even though works alone. Don't add but.中文說「雖然……但是……」，英文只用 Even though，不加 but。

From Outline to Essay

從大綱到文章
Introduction / conclusion · 引言／結論Reason (main point) · 理由（重點）Example or explanation · 例子或解釋Sentence pattern · 句型
Every Student Should Join a Club

Many students are busy with homework and cram school. However, I agree that every student should join a school club.

First of all, clubs help students make new friends. In a club, you meet people who like the same things as you. For example, in the basketball club, you practice with students from other classes.

Second, students can learn new skills. They might learn to play the guitar, take photos, or speak in front of people. These skills are not as easy to learn in normal classes.

Finally, clubs are a good way to relax. After a long day of tests, playing a sport or making art helps students feel less stressed.

Even though clubs take some time, they are worth it. Every student can find a club that makes school more fun.
