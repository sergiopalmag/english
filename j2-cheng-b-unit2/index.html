What Do Body Sentences Do?

內文句的工作是什麼？
✓
They tell more about the topic sentence.更詳細地說明主題句。
✓
They explain, describe, give reasons, or give examples.解釋、描述、給理由或舉例子。
✗
They don't talk about other topics.不談跟主題無關的事。
Example · 範例
Topic Taiwanese night markets are fun.
describe 描述Night markets are bright, busy, and loud.
example 舉例You can eat stinky tofu and oyster omelets.
explain 解釋The food is cheap, so you can try a lot.
off topic 離題My brother is in high school.

Main Points and Supporting Details

Supporting details support the main points. Ask: Why? How? For example?支持細節：支持重點。問自己：為什麼？怎麼做？例如？

From Notes to Sentences

從筆記到完整句子
An outline has short notes. A paragraph needs full sentences.大綱寫短筆記，段落要用完整的句子。
Who? 誰+verb 動詞+more: when? why? how? 更多資訊
Capital letter at the start, period at the end.句首大寫，句尾加句號。
Note · 筆記Full sentence · 完整句子
save energy→
You can save energy at home.
turn off the lights→
You can turn off the lights when you leave a room.
eyes get tired→
Your eyes get tired when you watch TV for a long time.
help me get a good job→
English can help me get a good job in the future.
